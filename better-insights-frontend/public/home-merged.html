What services does Better Insights provide?

We provide comprehensive digital marketing and consulting services, including Web Design, Google & Meta Ads, SEO, Social Media Marketing, Public Relations, and Branding.

How do you measure project success and ROI?

We establish clear, measurable KPIs prior to launch—including qualified lead volume, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, and organic search ranking growth—tracked through real-time dashboards.

What industries does Better Insights work with?

We have deep consulting and execution experience across Education, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Digital Commerce, Legal, Real Estate, and Technology sectors.

How does the onboarding and collaboration process work?

We begin with an in-depth discovery audit, provide a transparent roadmap and deliverables schedule, communicate through dedicated channels, and provide regular performance reviews.

Who owns our campaign data and accounts?

You retain 100% ownership over all advertising accounts, analytics dashboards, website code, and creative assets. We believe in complete transparency and data sovereignty.

Can you help us scale into international markets?

Yes. We manage multi-region campaigns and international SEO architectures helping brands expand across India, the Middle East, Europe, and the United States.

What is the typical timeframe to see results?

Paid advertising and landing page optimizations typically drive leads within the first two weeks, while technical SEO and organic ranking strategies show strong compounded growth within 60 to 90 days.
